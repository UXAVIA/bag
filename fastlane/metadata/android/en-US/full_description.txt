Watch your cold-storage balance without a seed phrase on your phone. Paste a zpub, an output descriptor, or the two lines of Bitkey's wallet export, and Bag tracks the balance over Tor - your keys never leave the device. Add manually entered balances for coins held elsewhere, see your net worth in up to 3 currencies, log your DCA buys and keep a live widget on your home screen.

Bitcoin-only. No altcoins. No ads. No accounts. No analytics. Your holdings never leave your device.

FREE

* Net worth in up to 3 fiat currencies (USD, EUR, GBP and 25 more)
* Live BTC price charts - 1D / 1W / 1M / 1Y / ALL-time
* DCA tracker with average buy price, cost basis and P&L
* Home screen widget with live price, chart and net worth
* Mempool fee estimates (sat/vB)
* Sats / BTC display toggle
* Dark and light theme

PRO - one-time purchase, no subscription

* Watch-only wallets: zpub (BIP84) single-sig, output descriptors and multisig - Bitkey 2-of-3, Sparrow and Nunchuk exports
* Manual balances for exchange or hardware holdings, summed into net worth
* Multi-wallet portfolio
* Tor routing via Orbot for full network privacy
* Custom Esplora server - point Bag at your own node
* Bitcoin Health Check - privacy score across all wallets: address reuse, UTXO hygiene, coinjoin coverage
* Sentinel: always-on balance monitoring with instant alerts for incoming and outgoing transactions
* Mempool fee alerts (above/below threshold)

PRIVACY & SECURITY

Only derived addresses ever reach the block explorer - your xpub, descriptor and balance stay on-device, encrypted at rest. Every network path can be routed through Tor (Orbot required) and fails closed if Tor is down. No phone-home, no telemetry, no third-party SDKs. Optional biometric lock.
