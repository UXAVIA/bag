Multisig wallets: add a watch-only wallet from an output descriptor, including Bitkey's 2-of-3. Single-sig zpubs work exactly as before.

Coins held elsewhere: add a plain amount for an exchange balance and it counts towards your net worth alongside your wallets.

Tap the balance on the home screen to jump to your holdings. The whole widget now opens the app.

Privacy: balances, your purchase log and your node address are now encrypted on device, and Tor is enforced on price requests on iOS.
